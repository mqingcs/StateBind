3 (Fig. 3) - An annotated instrument image shows an accurately localized target beside a displaced scale lattice. Two charts compare target and lattice localization within one tick and show larger Physicalized gains for two-endpoint ruler spans than for single-state tasks.
4 (Fig. 4) - A three-stage diagram uses the existing thermometer example: Doubao and Gemini propose targets and scale lattices, Qwen validates component evidence, and a two-by-two matrix exchanges target and lattice sources. All four valid recombinations choose B, 38.25 degrees Celsius. The precision gate accepts only four-way agreement with at least four-tick candidate separation; otherwise the system retains Numeric.
5 (Fig. 5) - Two panels show accuracy on the fixed 72-cell route and candidate precision at different state separations. Numeric reader results are compared with the automatic verifier on identical routed cells; stronger state separation raises precision among four-way agreements.
