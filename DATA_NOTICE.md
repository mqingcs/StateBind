# Data and image notice

StateBind-200 augments MeasureBench rows.  The compact release identifies every
source through `opaque_id` and `original_question_id` in
`data/source_index200.jsonl`, but does not redistribute the 200 benchmark
images.  Obtain the source dataset under its published terms and join on those
stable identifiers.  Geometry coordinates are released as research
annotations.

Three image assets used only to rebuild the paper figures are included under
the source attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 notice printed in the corresponding
paper captions.  They are not a substitute for the source dataset.

`r4_026__d4__swapped_order__physical.png`, and
